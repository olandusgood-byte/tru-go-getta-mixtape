# V1.4 City Life + Player Feedback

Additive slice on the existing V1.3/V1.4 systems.

- Expands repeatable City Events from 3 to 5 without replacing existing events.
- Adds availability/ready-state summaries and last-reward feedback.
- Preserves `tgg-events-v1`, existing APIs, inventory gating, district gating, crew bonuses, economy, and production gate.
- Adds deterministic browser smoke coverage for the new Night Market event and feedback state.
